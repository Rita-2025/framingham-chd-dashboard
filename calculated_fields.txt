CALCULATED FIELDS - CHD Risk Dashboard (Tableau)
Framingham Heart Study analysis by Rita Manzie
================================================

Formulas below are copied from the Tableau workbook
RITA_MANZIE_CHD_RISK_DASHBOARD.twbx.


1. Age Group
------------
Used in: Prevalence of CHD by Age Group (columns)

IF [age] < 30 THEN "Under 30"
ELSEIF [age] < 40 THEN "30-39"
ELSEIF [age] < 50 THEN "40-49"
ELSEIF [age] < 60 THEN "50-59"
ELSE "60+"
END

Note: participants aged 70-79 are merged into "60+" because only 2 people
fell in that range, too few to report a separate rate. No participants
were under 30, so that group does not appear in the chart.


2. % CHD Prevalence  (shown on the chart axis as "% with CHD")
----------------------------------------------------------------
Used in: Prevalence of CHD by Age Group (rows)

SUM([anyCHD]) / COUNT([anyCHD]) * 100

Note: anyCHD is 1 (developed CHD) or 0 (did not), so this is the share of
participants with CHD, shown as a percentage. Number format: custom,
1 decimal place, with a "%" suffix (the value is already multiplied by 100).


MEASURE SETTINGS (no formula needed)
====================================

CHD rate for smokers, non-smokers, men and women
  Field: Any CHD, aggregation set to AVERAGE.
  The average of a 0/1 field is the share with CHD.
  Number format: Percentage, 1 decimal place.

Scorecard averages
  Age, BMI, Sys BP, Dia BP, Heart Rate, Cigs Per Day: aggregation AVERAGE.

Scorecard percentages
  BP Meds, Diabetes, Prevalent Stroke, Prevalent Hyp: aggregation AVERAGE
  (0/1 fields, so the average is the share with the condition).
  Number format: Percentage, 1 decimal place.

BMI box plots
  Analysis > Aggregate Measures turned OFF, so each participant is one mark.
  19 participants with no BMI value are excluded.
